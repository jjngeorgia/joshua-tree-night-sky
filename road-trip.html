Palm Springs to Joshua Tree · Architecture drive

Modernism on the northbound road

A one-way drive from Palm Springs to Joshua Tree town, with a short downtown architecture stop and a public-street tour through a modernist neighborhood.

The drive

  1. Architecture and Design Center

    300 S Palm Canyon Drive, Palm Springs

    E. Stewart Williams designed this 1961 International Style building as the Santa Fe Federal Savings and Loan. Visit the exhibitions if time allows.

    30-60 min visitCheck museum hours

    Visitor details and hours

  2. Aluminaire House

    101 N Museum Drive, Palm Springs

    On the art museum campus, a short walk from the first stop. Designed by A. Lawrence Kocher and Albert Frey in 1931, this experimental all-metal house was first shown in New York and later reconstructed here. The exterior exhibit is free; the interior is not open.

    10-20 min on foot

    Museum visitor details

  3. Forever Marilyn

    Downtown Park, 134 N Museum Drive, Palm Springs

    A public 26-foot sculpture by Seward Johnson, near the art museum. Monroe's Palm Springs connection is real, but the often-repeated claim that she owned a home in Vista Las Palmas is not supported by the historical record. She visited the Racquet Club and was photographed there; this statue is the verified public landmark on this route.

    Public landmark

    Palm Springs history and fact check

  4. Twin Palms, Frank Sinatra House

    1148 E Alejo Road, Palm Springs

    E. Stewart Williams designed this Desert Modern home for Sinatra and his family after his 1947 commission. It is a private residence and event rental; view only from the public street. Do not enter, linger at the gate, or photograph residents.

    Private home · drive-by only

    Official Twin Palms history

  5. House of Tomorrow, Elvis Honeymoon Hideaway

    1350 Ladera Circle, Palm Springs

    William Krisel designed the home for Robert and Helene Alexander in 1960. Elvis and Priscilla Presley lived here and honeymooned here in 1967. It is private property; the owner currently lists tours as unavailable. View only from the public street.

    Private home · tours unavailable

    Owner's site and tour status

  6. Racquet Club Estates

    Neighborhood drive-through · Palm Springs

    See the postwar Alexander-built homes and low-profile desert-modern streetscape from public streets. Keep moving, don't enter private property, and avoid stopping for photos in front of homes.

    Drive-by only

  7. Tramway Gas Station

    2901 N Palm Canyon Drive, Palm Springs

    Albert Frey and Robson C. Chambers designed this 1965 former gas station, known for its folded, cantilevered roof. It is listed on the National Register and now serves as the Palm Springs Visitor Center.

    Exterior landmarkCheck access before visiting

    National Register record

  8. Joshua Tree Visitor Center

    6554 Park Boulevard, Joshua Tree

    A clear end point in town, with a bookstore, cafe, and restrooms. Check current hours and park conditions before continuing into the park.

    National Park Service visitor information
